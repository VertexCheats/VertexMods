Tutorials

A quick, step-by-step walkthrough for installing Vertex mods — plus the easiest way to manage them.

1

Download the mod

Head to the homepage or Downloads page and click the download button on any mod card. The file saves straight from storage as a .jar — no installer, no bundled extras.

meteor-client.jar 2.1 MB
2

Drop it into your mods folder

Open your .minecraft/mods folder and drag the downloaded .jar in. Using the Modrinth App instead? It handles this step for you automatically.

meteor-client.jar
mods 0 files Added to mods folder ✓
3

Launch and enable it

Start Minecraft from the Fabric profile in your launcher. The mod appears in your mod list, ready to switch on — no extra configuration needed for most mods.
